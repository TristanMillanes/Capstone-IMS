Data Privacy & Security Governance

By submitting an access request to the Provincial Government Environment and Natural Resources Office (PGENRO) IMS, you agree to comply with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

Clearance & Security Provisions:

  • Credential Protection: Assigned credentials are exclusively for the authorized individual. Account sharing is strictly prohibited.
  • Division Boundaries: Access is limited to your endorsed Division (Admin, Environment, Forestry, or Mines & Quarry).
  • System Audit Trails: All database submissions, exports, and document views are permanently logged and monitored by the System Administrator.

Unauthorized access or falsification of endorsement letters is subject to administrative sanctions under Civil Service rules.
