REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10173

Environmental Data Privacy Governance

As a verified operator of the Provincial Government Environment and Natural Resources Office (PGENRO) Information Management System, you are entrusted with confidential spatial, ecological, and industrial concession data.

Mandatory Operator Obligations

  • Non-Disclosure of Concession Maps: Geospatial coordinates of pending mineral quarry applications, active watershed reserves, and endangered flora sanctuaries must not be exported without explicit written clearance.
  • Account Non-Transferability: Credentials assigned are personal to your civil service appointment. Sharing passwords or permitting unauthorized operators to access your terminal constitutes an offense under civil service regulations.
  • Audit Trail Integrity: All queries, record alterations, export timestamps, and IP addresses are recorded permanently in the PGENRO central security audit repository.

By confirming this declaration, you accept liability under Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act) and the Environmental Protection Code of the Province.
